# Healthcare Data Analytics Dashboard – Hospital Performance & Patient Outcomes

## Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end Healthcare Analytics solution developed in Power BI to analyze hospital performance, patient outcomes, discharge activity, and cost efficiency across healthcare facilities.

The project focused on transforming healthcare operational data into actionable insights through interactive dashboards that enable stakeholders to compare hospitals, monitor patient outcomes, and identify opportunities for improving healthcare delivery.

The solution incorporated data modeling, DAX calculations, KPI development, and advanced visualizations to provide a comprehensive view of healthcare performance metrics such as Length of Stay (LOS), cost per discharge, patient risk factors, diagnosis trends, and hospital-level performance.

---

## Business Goals

The primary objectives of the project were to:

- Monitor hospital performance across healthcare facilities.
- Compare hospital metrics against state-wide benchmarks.
- Analyze patient outcomes based on severity of illness and risk of mortality.
- Identify drivers of higher treatment costs and longer hospital stays.
- Improve operational efficiency through data-driven decision-making.
- Support healthcare administrators in resource allocation and performance management.
- Enable detailed hospital-level analysis through interactive reporting.

## Key Insights Enabled

The dashboard enabled stakeholders to:

- Compare hospital performance against state-wide healthcare benchmarks.
- Identify hospitals with significantly higher or lower costs per discharge.
- Monitor variations in patient length of stay across facilities.
- Analyze discharge patterns by diagnosis, severity of illness, and mortality risk.
- Detect operational inefficiencies contributing to extended patient stays.
- Understand patient disposition outcomes across healthcare providers.
- Support evidence-based decision-making for healthcare administrators and hospital leadership.
- Perform facility-level performance reviews using interactive hospital profiles.
